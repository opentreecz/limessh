# R-019 — Jump host / ProxyJump connect-via (C-02)

## Findings

JuiceSSH: `Connection.via` FK, chain walked by `ai0.h()`, jump engine opens local port forward to target, target client connects to localhost:localPort.

SSHJ 0.40.0: `SSHClient extends SocketClient`; `SocketClient.connectVia(DirectConnection)` chains the target transport through a jump-host SSH channel. `SSHClient.newDirectConnection(host, port)` returns `DirectConnection`.
